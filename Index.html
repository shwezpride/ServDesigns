// 06 — Founder

Built on real delivery experience, not just a viewpoint.

"Don't just fix the experience. Understand the system that created it."

"The visible problem is rarely the whole problem," says Shweta. "A difficult customer journey is often an operational issue. A slow process is often a technology issue. A technology issue is often an organisational one. We look at the whole system — not just the part that's easiest to see."

SA

Shweta Amale

Founder, ServDesigns

Founded by Shweta Amale, a human-centred service designer who has spent 15+ years working across design, technology and transformation — including large-scale programmes in financial services, healthcare and the public sector.

Her background bridges engineering, consulting and a Service Design MA from the Royal College of Art — design, business and technology, not just the interface on top of them.

ServDesigns brings that same cross-disciplinary thinking to smaller teams and ambitious businesses — helping them understand complex problems, design practical, evidence-based solutions, and stay involved until those solutions work in the real world.
